Campus Marketplace Policy

Terms & Conditions

These terms govern listings, bidding, payments, messaging, moderation, and account use on Gavel.

Eligibility

Gavel is intended for students and campus community members using accurate account information. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for all activity that occurs under your account.

Bidding Rules

Bids are binding. If you place a bid or configure auto-bid, you authorize Gavel to treat that amount as a live commitment. Anti-snipe extensions, reserve logic, and approval workflows are enforced by the platform.

Seller Duties

Sellers must own or be authorized to sell the listed item, disclose defects honestly, and upload real media of the actual product. Counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, illegal, or prohibited goods are not allowed.

Payments And Settlement

Wallet deposits, holds, refunds, and auction settlements may be applied automatically. Winning bidders must complete payment and collection within the stated timeline. Repeated non-payment may result in suspension.

Disputes

If a product is materially different from its listing, both parties must cooperate with platform review. Gavel may inspect messages, media, and moderation notes when resolving disputes. Final outcomes may include reversal, warning, suspension, or permanent removal.

Account Suspension

Gavel may pause or terminate accounts for fraud, harassment, charge abuse, manipulation of bids, repeated cancelled transactions, or attempts to bypass review and safety processes.
