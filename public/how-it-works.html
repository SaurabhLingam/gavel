Campus Commerce

How Gavel Works

Gavel is a student marketplace for auctions and direct product inquiries. Discover items in reels, bid live, message sellers, and close campus meetups with trust and moderation built in.

1. Discover

Browse the explore feed for short videos or open the auctions page for list and grid browsing. Use condition filters, saved searches, and watchlists to track the products you care about.

2. Bid Or Message

Place a live bid, configure auto-bid, or open a product chat to ask for more images, pricing details, or meetup information before committing.

3. Close Safely

Winners get a follow-up flow to contact the seller, schedule a campus meetup, confirm delivery, and leave a review that affects trust score.

Selling Step By Step

Create a listing with photos, a short walkthrough video, condition details, description, and specs. Preview the listing before submitting. Admins review media and claims before the item goes live.

Seller View

Upload clear images, add accurate description/specs, preview, then submit for review.

Buyer View

See the same listing as buyers do: cover image, countdown, description, specs, chat, and bid actions.
