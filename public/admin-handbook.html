Operations Manual

Admin Handbook

Admins review listings, protect bidding integrity, document moderation decisions, and escalate risky cases to super admin.

Core Responsibilities

Review the actual listing content, verify category and claims, confirm usable media, and reject anything misleading or incomplete.

Use audit-ready notes. Every approval or rejection should explain what was observed and why the decision was taken.

Disputes And Escalation

Escalate suspected fraud, counterfeit items, threats, manipulated media, or repeat seller abuse to super admin immediately. Do not guess on high-risk cases.

How To Review Listings

Check description completeness, compare media with claims, confirm condition disclosures, verify category fit, and reorder images if a clearer presentation helps buyers understand the item.

Audit Trail

Gavel records assignment events, decisions, notes, and notifications. Admins should write notes assuming the seller, super admin, and dispute reviewers may read them later.

Become an Admin

Applicants should be reliable, high-trust users who can review listings consistently and without conflicts of interest.
